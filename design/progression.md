# Core + Discipline Progression (Levels 1–100)

## Design Goals
- Holy Trinity preserved (Tank / Healer / DPS) but with soft flexibility.
- Avoid button bloat and late-game power spikes.
- Skills change function at milestones instead of just getting bigger numbers.
- Disciplines give dual-class flavor without full multiclass imbalance.

## Cores (Pick 1 at Level 1)
| Core | Primary Role | Fantasy | Base Strengths |
|------|--------------|---------|----------------|
| **Warden** | Tank | Protector who controls space | High defense, threat, mitigation |
| **Channeler** | Healer | Support who sustains allies | Healing, shields, utility |
| **Striker** | DPS (Melee/Ranged) | Damage dealer with precision or momentum | High damage, mobility, crit |
| **Invoker** | DPS (Caster) | Elemental or summon specialist | Skill damage, AoE, control |

## Disciplines (Unlock slots at 10, 25, 50, 75)
You can have up to **2 active disciplines** at once.  
Swapping a discipline requires a rest or town (or a long cooldown).

### Example Disciplines
- **Precision** (Striker/Invoker) – Higher single-target, weaker AoE
- **Momentum** (Striker) – Builds speed and chains attacks
- **Bulwark** (Warden) – Extra mitigation and shields
- **Sanctuary** (Channeler) – Stronger single-target heals and cleanses
- **Tempest** (Invoker) – Lightning/fire focus with higher burst
- **Wild** (Any) – Nature-themed utility and sustain
- **Shadow** (Striker/Invoker) – Stealth, poison, or control tools
- **Aegis** (Warden/Channeler) – Hybrid tank-healer support

| Level | Unlock Type | Notes |
|-------|-------------|-------|

## Example Progression: Striker Core
**Level 1**
- Quick Strike (basic attack)
- Evade (short dash)
- Focus (small damage buff)

**Level 20** – Quick Strike gains a follow-up if it crits  
**Level 35** – Evade leaves a short speed buff  
**Level 50** – New skill: Chain Assault (hits 2–3 targets in a line)  
**Level 65** – Quick Strike follow-up can apply a bleed or elemental based on discipline  
**Level 80** – Chain Assault gains extra hit on primary target  
**Level 100** – Capstone: Momentum Surge (short window of greatly increased attack speed)

## Scaling Notes
- Damage and healing scale with level + gear, but cooldowns or resource costs on strong effects increase slightly after level 50.
- Disciplines modify existing skills more than they add new buttons.
- Tank threat and mitigation scale more linearly; DPS burst is front-loaded then plateaus.

## Balance Levers
- Strong effects have growing cooldowns or resource costs after level 60.
- Discipline combinations are limited (you can’t stack two pure damage disciplines without losing utility).
- Role identity is preserved by core stats and a few locked abilities.
