## Scaling Observations
- **Tank items** (Ironheart, Sacred Bastion, Thornweave, Arreat’s) scale well into late game because mitigation and defense remain relevant.
- **Pure DPS items** (Bloodforge, Soulreaver, Stormweaver) risk becoming too strong after level 70 unless cooldowns or resource costs grow.
- **Healer items** are currently underrepresented. Eclipse Ward and Frostveil help, but more Channeler-focused essences would improve balance.
- Most class-specific uniques map cleanly to one core. Their alternate versions already shift focus in ways that match different disciplines.

## Recommendations
1. Add 2–3 more Channeler (healer) essences.
2. Add soft level-scaling costs to high-damage essences after level 60.
3. Create 1–2 hybrid essences that work for Aegis (tank-healer) discipline.
